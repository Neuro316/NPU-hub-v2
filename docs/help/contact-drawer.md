## Steps
1. Open CRM, then Contacts, and choose a contact to open their drawer.
2. Choose Timeline to see what happened with them, in order.
3. Choose Consent to see, for email and text, whether they may receive marketing and service messages, and the record each answer comes from.
4. To record a decision made outside a form, such as on a call, use Record a decision made outside a form: choose the channel, the kind, and whether they agreed or withdrew, write what they agreed to, and press Save record.
5. Choose Campaigns to see where they came from, which campaigns they are in, and the send decisions made for them.

## Common mistakes
- The Consent tab reads the consent record, not the older yes or no fields on the contact.
- Write the words the person actually agreed to. An agreement with no wording cannot be saved.
- A withdrawal takes effect straight away and stops the messages it covers.
