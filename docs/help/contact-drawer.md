## Steps
1. Open CRM, then Contacts, and choose a contact to open their drawer.
2. Choose Timeline to see what happened with them, in order.
3. Choose Consent to see, for email and text, whether they may receive marketing and service messages, and the record each answer comes from.
4. To record a decision made outside a form, such as on a call, use Record a decision made outside a form: choose the channel, the kind, and whether they agreed or withdrew, write what they agreed to, and press Save record.
5. Choose Campaigns to see where they came from, which campaigns they are in, and the send decisions made for them.
6. Above Communications, Recent email lists their last 7 email conversations across the connected mailboxes, newest first: the subject, the date, the mailbox and who replied last. It never shows the message itself. A conversation in someone else's private mailbox shows only as 1 private conversation with its date. With no email conversations, the block does not appear.
7. A Waiting on us line at the top of the card means the person has been left waiting: a booking link with no call booked after 2 days, an email or a text with no reply after a day, coursework quiet for 7 days, or no follow-up 2 days after a discovery call. Open card goes to its card in Follow-ups to review on the board. The line and the card clear on their own once the person is answered or comes back.
8. Texts get a faster alert as well. When someone's text has had no reply by text or call for an hour, it is listed in the Slack channel #np-follow-ups. The touchpoint check runs every 30 minutes and posts one message listing every waiting text, newest first: the name (or the number), the line (Primary or WNW Office), the start of their text, how long it has waited, and a link to the thread in Conversations. Each one says New the first time and Still waiting after that. It drops off once anyone replies by text or calls them, and a new text after a reply starts the clock again. Nothing posts before 8 AM Eastern, so overnight texts appear in the 8 AM post. The first alert for each text adds a Text waiting on a reply line to the contact's timeline. Test, staff and do-not-contact contacts are never listed.

## Common mistakes
- The Consent tab reads the consent record, not the older yes or no fields on the contact.
- Write the words the person actually agreed to. An agreement with no wording cannot be saved.
- A withdrawal takes effect straight away and stops the messages it covers.
