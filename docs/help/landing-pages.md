## Steps
1. Open Forms and Pages and scroll to Landing pages.
2. Press New page, give it a title, and choose a page address in lower case letters, numbers and single dashes.
3. Build the page from blocks: heading, text, list, image, button or form. Use the arrow to move a block up.
4. To collect sign-ups, add a form block and choose which sign-up form it shows.
5. Set the status to Published and press Save page.
6. Once pages are switched on for your organization, press Open the live page to see it.

## Common mistakes
- A page cannot be published while its form is still a draft. Publish the form first.
- A published page stays hidden until a superadmin switches pages on in CRM Settings.
- Images and outside links must use an https address.
- A page drafted by the Campaign Builder shows "AI draft, needs review" until you approve it or save it.
