## Steps
1. Open Forms and Pages from the sidebar and press New form.
2. Give it a Name and a Form address, using lower case letters, numbers and single dashes.
3. Check the Fields. Each field can save to the contact's first name, last name, email or phone.
4. Write each Consent box exactly as the visitor will see it, and choose its channel (email or text) and kind (marketing or service).
5. Leave Starts campaigns routed from empty to use form:<form address>, or type a different source key.
6. Set the Status to Published when it is ready, then press Save form.
7. Copy the Embed on a website snippet into the page where the form will live.
8. On the campaign it should start, add this form under Starts from.

## Common mistakes
- A published form needs a way to create a contact (an email or phone field) and wording for every consent box, or it cannot be published.
- Forms accept submissions only while form intake is switched on for your organization.
- Keep an empty hidden field named website on the page. Real people never fill it in, and submissions that do are dropped.
- Changing a published form gives it a new version; earlier submissions keep the version they answered.
