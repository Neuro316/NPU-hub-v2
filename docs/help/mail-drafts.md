## Steps
1. Open Inbox. It opens on the Needs review tab, which lists emails with a draft ready and questions that need a person. The number on the tab, and beside Inbox in the sidebar, is how many are waiting. All lists every email; Learning shows what the drafts have learned.
2. Each email shows a badge: Draft ready, In Gmail as a draft, Rejected or Needs a person. Click an email to open the review pane beside the list. On a phone it fills the screen; Back to the list closes it.
3. The pane shows the email first: subject, sender, mailbox, time, the AI summary and why it has that priority. Below it, What I looked at lists the Hub contact, pipeline and stage, University status, calendar and how many recent threads came from this sender. It never quotes the email.
4. The draft is read live from Gmail, with its From and To, and a line on why it was written that way.
5. Approve marks the draft approved and opens it in Gmail. Press Send in Gmail yourself.
6. Edit turns the draft into a text box. Approve my version saves your words into the same Gmail draft. It still does not send. After you send it from Gmail, What I learned from this one shows the lesson. Until then it says the lesson is pending: lessons are written at 20 and 50 minutes past each hour.
7. Redraft writes a new draft, taking account of an optional one-line note, for example "shorter, and mention the welcome email". The new draft replaces the old one in Gmail. If the email turns out to be clinical, legal, about money or a possible crisis, no draft is written and it becomes Needs a person.
8. Reject asks for a reason: wrong tone, wrong facts, should not reply or I will handle it. You can add one line under 200 characters, with no @. The Gmail draft is deleted, and your reason becomes a lesson the next drafts read.
9. Needs a person shows why (clinical, legal, financial or crisis) and has no draft. Answer it yourself in Gmail, then press I answered it, dismiss.
10. Click any priority pill to change it to Urgent, Normal or Low, choose a reason (deadline, client, lead, partner, not important) and press Save. The pill then says set by you. Triage follows your changes for later mail from the same sender in the same category.
11. On the Learning tab you see a 30 day scorecard for each mailbox: drafted, approved as written, edited, rejected and needs a person. Below it are the lessons, newest first. Pin keeps a lesson in every future draft; Retire stops it being used. Last is the log of priority corrections.

## Common mistakes
- The Hub never sends an email. A draft sits in Gmail until you send it there.
- The draft's words live only in Gmail. The Hub shows them by reading Gmail while the pane is open. It keeps a pointer to the draft and one short lesson sentence, never the email or the draft text.
- You see drafts and lessons only for your own mailboxes, and for shared mailboxes if you are a superadmin. A private mailbox's drafts and lessons show only to its owner.
- Thank-you notes, our own replies and automated notices never get a draft.
- Drafting is off until it is turned on for the organization. While it is off, Needs review stays empty.
