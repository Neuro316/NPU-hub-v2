## Steps
1. Open Brief in the sidebar to see what your next 8 AM text says, built from this moment.
2. Each line is a count with a link: new leads since the last brief, follow-ups due today and overdue, clients stuck in onboarding, today's discovery calls and coaching, tasks flagged urgent for you, and new mail in your own mailbox.
3. Press Open on a line to go to that page in the Hub.

## Common mistakes
- In Preview nothing is texted. The page shows what the text would say.
- The brief never goes out before 8 AM Eastern or on a weekend. Urgent items still text right away.
- Mail is shown as a count of messages received since the last brief, never with a subject.
