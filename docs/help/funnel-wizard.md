## Steps
1. On the Funnels tab, press New funnel campaign.
2. What it is for: choose the purpose closest to yours and give the campaign a name. A note for your team is optional.
3. Who enters: choose what brings a person into the campaign, such as submitting a form, and press Add this. You can add more than one.
4. Pipeline: choose the pipeline, the stage people enter at, and the stage where the goal is reached. A pipeline is optional.
5. Messages: start from a template or add a blank step, then write each message and press Preview to see it as a person would.
6. Review and test: work through the readiness list. Anything not ready links to where it is set.
7. Press Save as draft to keep it without running, or Save, set Active and test drive to switch it on and send your own test contact through it.

## Common mistakes
- Next stays disabled until the campaign has a name.
- A source shown as not connected yet will not start the campaign until it is connected.
- Setting the campaign to Active lets real people who arrive from its sources enter it. While live sending is off, they only get dry runs.
- On a draft campaign, Run the guided setup again reopens these steps with what you already chose.
