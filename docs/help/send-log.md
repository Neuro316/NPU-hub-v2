## Steps
1. Open the contact and choose the Campaigns tab.
2. Under Send decisions, each line shows when, the channel and kind, the decision, and the reason.
3. Allow means the message was sent, or recorded as a dry run when the line says dry run.
4. Refused means it was not sent; the reason says why, such as no agreement to marketing on that channel.
5. Waiting means it is outside the person's send window and will try again when the window opens.
6. For a test drive, the campaign page shows the same decision with what was sent or would have been sent.

## Common mistakes
- Refused for the marketing limit protects the person from too many messages; it is not a fault.
- A message the Hub is unsure was delivered is not tried again, to avoid sending it twice.
- Message wording is only shown for test contacts on the campaign page.
