## Steps
1. On the Campaigns hub, press Campaign Builder. Only a platform superadmin sees it.
2. Describe what you want to accomplish, such as giving away a guide and then inviting people to book a call. You can paste an example email for reference.
3. Press Draft it, then read what it will create on the review screen.
4. Press Build to save it as a draft campaign, or describe changes and draft again.
5. Open the new campaign. Each item it wrote shows AI draft, needs review until you edit or approve it.
6. Work through the follow-up tasks it listed, which also appear in Client Tasks.
7. Activate the campaign yourself when it is ready. Its starting sources switch on with it.

## Common mistakes
- The Builder never sends, goes live, enrolls anyone or changes consent. It only drafts.
- Messages the claims check flagged are saved anyway and marked needs review, with a task saying why.
- Asking it to change a live campaign creates a new draft copy; the live one is untouched.
