## Steps
1. Open Campaigns from the sidebar and choose the Funnels tab.
2. The list on the left shows each campaign with its status, how many steps it has, how many people are in it now and how many reached the goal.
3. Choose a campaign to open it on the right, where you can change its name, status, pipeline, what starts it, its steps and how it sends.
4. Press New funnel campaign to set one up with the guided setup.
5. Change the Status to Active when the campaign is ready to take in people, then press Save campaign.

## Common mistakes
- A Draft campaign does nothing at all. People only enter an Active campaign.
- Paused holds everyone where they are; Archived puts the campaign away. Neither sends anything.
- Changes apply to people who enter from now on. People already in the campaign keep the steps they started with until they reach the next step.
- If the campaign engine is switched off for your organization, you can still build campaigns, but nothing runs until a superadmin switches it on in CRM Settings, Campaign Sending.
