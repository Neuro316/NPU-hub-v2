## Steps
1. Open CRM, then Pipelines, and choose the pipeline at the top.
2. Each column is a stage. Drag a contact's card to move them to another stage.
3. Press Edit Stages to add, rename or reorder stages.
4. Open the pipeline menu at the top and choose New Pipeline to start a new one, optionally from a template.
5. In a funnel campaign, choose this pipeline, the stage people enter at and the stage where the goal is reached.

## Common mistakes
- Renaming a stage keeps it the same stage, so campaigns that use it keep working.
- Removing a stage puts it away rather than deleting it; campaigns that pointed at it should be updated.
- A stage can have its own stage emails. Dragging a card into that stage sends them, separately from any campaign.
- When a campaign's goal stage is reached, the campaign stops sending that person its messages.
