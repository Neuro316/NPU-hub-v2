## Steps
1. Open University Access from the sidebar and press Add an asset.
2. Give it a Title, the Page on university.neuroprogeny.com it should open, such as a free lesson's sign-up page, and a short description.
3. Keep Available to campaigns ticked, then press Save asset.
4. In a campaign's steps, set What it does to Deliver a free University asset and choose the asset.
5. Write the message. The private link goes where you write {{asset_link}}, or at the end.

## Common mistakes
- The page must be on university.neuroprogeny.com; other addresses are refused.
- Links are only sent while delivery is switched on for your organization.
- Each link is private to one person and expires, so it cannot be shared around.
- An asset that is switched off can no longer be chosen for new steps.
