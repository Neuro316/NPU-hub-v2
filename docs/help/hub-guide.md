## Steps
1. On the Campaigns hub, press Hub Guide.
2. Type your question, such as how to test a campaign, and press Ask.
3. Read the answer and the articles it cites.
4. When it offers steps, press Take me there to open the right page, and Show me to highlight the control to use.
5. Do each step yourself; the Guide never clicks or saves for you.

## Common mistakes
- The Guide only knows the help articles. When none fits, it says so plainly and marks its general pointer as not from the help set.
- It sees only which page you are on, never what is on the page, so describe what you are trying to do.
- To have something built for you, a superadmin can switch to the Campaign Builder.
