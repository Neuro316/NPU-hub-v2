## Steps
1. Press the round compass button in the bottom right corner of any page. On the Campaigns hub you can also press Hub Guide.
2. Type your question, such as how to test a campaign, and press Ask.
3. Read the answer and the articles it cites. The panel shows which step you are on.
4. Press Take me there to open the right page; the panel stays open and keeps your place.
5. Press Show me to highlight the control to use, do the step yourself, then press Done, next step.
6. Press Start over to clear the answer, or close the panel and reopen it later in the same tab to carry on.

## Common mistakes
- The Guide only knows the help articles. When none fits, it says so plainly and marks its general pointer as not from the help set.
- It sees only which page you are on, never what is on the page, so describe what you are trying to do.
- Your place is kept for this browser tab only, and it is cleared when you sign out or switch organization.
- To have something built for you, a superadmin can switch to the Campaign Builder tab.
