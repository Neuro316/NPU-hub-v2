## Steps
1. People agree by ticking a consent box on a published form. The Hub records the exact wording they saw.
2. You can record an agreement made another way in the contact's Consent tab, with the words they agreed to.
3. Each marketing email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Using it withdraws marketing email at once.
4. Replying STOP to a text withdraws texts at once. Replying START brings back service texts only.
5. Before every send, the Hub checks agreement, do not contact, bounces and complaints, the send window and the marketing limit, and records the reason for each decision.

## Common mistakes
- Agreeing to service messages does not cover marketing.
- A form can only add agreement. It never reverses a person's own earlier withdrawal.
- An address that bounced or complained stays blocked even if they later agree again.
- A text agreement counts only for the number the person typed on the form.
