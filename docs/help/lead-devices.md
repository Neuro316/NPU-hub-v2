## Steps
1. Open Lead Devices under GROW.
2. Choose Upload HTML and pick one self-contained file of up to 2 MB, or choose Form only. Fill in the name and the address, then press Create device.
3. Open the device and check the pipeline, lists and campaigns created for it.
4. Press Approve and serve on the version you want people to see.
5. Open the test link and send a test submission. It is stored as a test and creates no contact. Press Show submissions on the device to see what was entered; tests are labelled, and their details are removed after 30 days.
6. Review the emails, then press Approve emails. Run your test drive, then press Mark test drive passed.
7. Cameron presses Go live when all three readiness items are ticked.
8. Share the public link, or paste the embed code into a page.

## Common mistakes
- A submission marked Contact not created kept the details typed. Press Retry on it once the problem is fixed; it works only while the device is live.
- A file never asks for an email or phone number. It calls window.NPU.submit with its answers, and the Hub lead form collects the person's details.
- Uploading a new version changes nothing until you approve it, so the live link keeps showing the approved version.
- The public link shows not available until the device is live. Use the test link until then.
- Going live does not switch on real sending. Each campaign still has its own live switch, and the organization's live sending must be on.
- A submission to a device that is not live is a test. It creates no contact, even from an external site.
