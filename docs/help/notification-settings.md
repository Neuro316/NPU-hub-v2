## Steps
1. Open Notifications in the sidebar. Only org admins can open it.
2. For each notification, choose the recipient and when: Urgent texts now, 8 AM brief adds it to the morning text, Off drops it.
3. Set the 8 AM brief to Off or Preview. Preview shows the brief on the Brief page without sending anything.
4. Press Save. If the save fails, the page says so.

## Common mistakes
- Live is not offered here. It is switched on separately after a preview has been checked.
- The speed-to-lead alerts, the Monday scorecard text and the watchdog keep their own settings and keep working as before.
- A recipient must be active staff of this organization.
