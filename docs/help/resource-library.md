## Steps
1. Open Resources in the sidebar. It is for platform superadmins.
2. Search by title or type, or choose a type, to narrow the list of files and links.
3. To add a link, fill in Add a link: a title, the full https address, the type, the owner and, if you like, where it is used. Press Add link.
4. Edit a link's title, address, type or where used and press Save. Choosing an owner saves straight away.
5. Under each resource, "In campaign steps" lists the campaign steps whose subject or body contains its address. The Hub finds these itself.
6. Press Retire to take a resource out of use. Show retired lists retired ones, and Restore brings one back.
7. To share one with a contact, open the contact's card, choose it under Share a resource, and press Make link. Press Copy and paste the link into your own email or text.
8. The card lists the links made for that contact, with when each was first and last opened. Each first open is also added to the contact's timeline.

## Common mistakes
- Make link sends nothing. The contact only sees the link once you paste it into a message you send yourself.
- A retired resource's tracked links stop working until it is restored. Emailed download links from Send resources keep working until they expire.
- Only full https addresses are accepted, and the same address can be in the library once.
- A tracked link opens for anyone who has it, and it counts every open, not just the contact's.
